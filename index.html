How to play & keyboard controls

1. Aim. Tap the stadium, move your mouse, or adjust the direction slider.

2. Charge. Hold the launch button or pull the ripcord right. One second reaches full power. Release to commit; Escape cancels.

3. Battle. Both tops launch together. A spin-out, an exit-pocket ring-out, or the most spin after 20 seconds decides the round.

Launch both: prepare coral, then teal. Keyboard: focus the arena, use left/right arrows to aim, hold Space to charge, and release to launch. Reset starts a fresh round.
